# Any other system — reading a night audit you have not seen before

Used when a hotel's front-desk system has no built-in reader and the AI helper
is asked to read a page. These are the rules every night audit follows, whoever
made it.

## Find the right page

- The figures live on a **summary** page: a journal, transaction or revenue
  summary, a trial balance, or a manager's or statistics report.
- Detail pages that list guests, rooms, folios or cards are never the right
  page. The app does not send those pages at all.

## Which night

A night audit covers **one business night**. Look for "Business Date", "Date
Range", "Audit Date" or a date beside the report title. A report printed the
next morning may show both; the business date is the one that matters.

## Reading a line

- Each money line has a **transaction code** (short letters or a number), a
  **description**, and one or more **amounts**.
- When there are several amount columns, use the **total** column for the night —
  not month to date, not year to date.
- A figure **in brackets**, or with a trailing or leading minus, is **negative**.
- Headings, subtotals and grand totals are not lines of their own.

## What a line is

- **Revenue:** room charges, fees, food and drink, parking, retail.
- **Taxes:** occupancy, sales, tourism and similar — money held for the
  government, never revenue.
- **Payments:** cash, cards, direct bill — money coming in, never revenue.
- **Adjustments and corrections** belong to the code they correct.

If a line's meaning is unclear, say so rather than guess. The owner confirms
every line before it reaches the books.
