# choiceADVANTAGE (SkyTouch) — reading the Standard Audit Pack

The night audit arrives as **one PDF holding many reports**, each starting on a
new page with its title on the first line. Only two of them carry the figures
the books need; the rest (A/R Aging, Guest Ledger, In House List, No Show
Report and so on) are read past.

## Which hotel

Every page header prints `Property Name:` and `Property Code:`. The **code** is
what identifies the hotel — a brand name such as "Comfort Inn" is printed on
many hotels' reports, a code on one.

## Which night

The header prints either `Business Date: M/D/YYYY` or
`Date Range: M/D/YYYY - M/D/YYYY`. For a night audit both dates in the range
are the same night.

## Hotel Journal Summary — the money

- The heading row reads `Description (Transaction Code) Postings Corrections
  Adjustments Totals`, followed by ledger columns.
- Each line is a description, its **code in brackets**, then the amounts:
  `Room Charge (RM) 7,147.07 0.00 0.00 7,147.07`.
- Use the **Totals** column — postings plus corrections plus adjustments.
- A figure **in brackets is negative**: `Visa Payment (VI) (2,406.13)` is money
  going out of the guest ledger — a payment, not revenue.
- Taxes appear as their own codes (for example a state occupancy tax). They are
  money held for the government, never revenue.
- The heading row itself is not a line: "Transaction Code" is two words, not a
  code.

## Hotel Statistics — the rooms

- Sections (Room Statistics, Performance Statistics) repeat a heading with the
  night's date, `PTD`, `Last Year PTD`, `YTD`, `Last YTD`.
- The first number on a line is the **night itself**; then period to date,
  last year's period to date, year to date, last year's year to date.
- `Total Rooms` is the size of the hotel on that night; it is where the app
  takes the room count from.

## Never

Guest names, room numbers with guests, card numbers and folio details appear on
other reports in the pack. They are never needed and are never read.
