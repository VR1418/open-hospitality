Everything is in the download

There is nothing else to install. No database to set up, no runtime to chase, no account to create with us — the download contains its own database and everything else it needs, and it all runs on your computer.

It reads your reports

Oracle OPERA, AutoClerk and choiceADVANTAGE, including whole audit packs. On another system, an AI helper you supply can read the report instead — and you confirm what it found.

It keeps real books

A posted general ledger, not a spreadsheet. Corrections are reversals, periods close, and the statement is built from the journal — so the numbers tie, and it tells you when they don't.

Every hotel at once

Last night's revenue, occupancy, ADR and RevPAR across the group, what the night audit turned up, and which charge codes still need a decision.

Installing it

  1. Unzip it somewhere with a short path. C:\Open Hospitality is ideal. Windows still refuses file names over 260 characters, and unzipping into a deeply nested folder can silently leave a few files behind.
  2. Double-click Open Hospitality.exe. It opens in its own window, a ◆ icon appears near the clock, and a Desktop icon is made for next time. First start takes under a minute while it creates your database.
  3. Create your account and save the recovery code. It is shown once. If you forget your password and lose that code, nobody can open your books — not even us. There is no reset email, because there is no email.
  4. Put your night audit PDFs in “Drop reports here”. Each is read within a few seconds — or let it collect them from your email every morning. Then open Overview.

Windows will warn you the first time.

A blue box saying “Windows protected your PC”. That is expected: the app is not signed yet, because signing means buying a certificate and that has not been done during testing. It is not a virus warning — Windows is saying it does not recognise the publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway. It only happens once.

Your figures stay on your computer

  • No account with us, no sign-up, no licence check.
  • No telemetry. The app does not report back what you do with it.
  • Your books live in a database on your machine. Backups go to a folder you choose — ideally one your cloud drive already syncs.
  • The only part that can reach the internet is the AI helper, and only if you switch it on and supply your own AI account. It is off to begin with, it has a monthly spending limit, and it is never shown a guest's name, a pay rate, a bank detail or an account number.

What it does not do yet

  • Mac. Windows only for now.
  • Signing. Hence the warning above.
  • Live bank feeds and payroll filing. You download a statement and it checks it; it prepares a pay period and hands it to your payroll provider.
  • Tax. This is not a tax product and will not advise on tax.

This is early software being tried by a handful of hotels. If something goes wrong, the log it keeps holds no guest names and no money — only what the app did — and sending it is the most useful thing you can do.
